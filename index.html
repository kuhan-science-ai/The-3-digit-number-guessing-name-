Rules guide

How to read each clue

Correct digit, correct place

If the secret is 479 and you guess 489, the 4 and 9 are counted as correct-place digits because they sit in the exact same slots.

Correct digit, wrong place

If the secret is 479 and you guess 914, the 9 and 4 belong to the code, but both are in different positions.

No repeated digits in Classic

Classic, Hard, and Time Attack use unique digits. A guess like 447 is not valid there because 4 repeats.

Use elimination

If a clue says none of your guessed digits are in the code, cross those digits out and build the next guess from fresh digits.
